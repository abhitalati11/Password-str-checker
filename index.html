Length

Longer passwords create a larger search space. This project gives the strongest weight to password length.

02

Entropy

Entropy is estimated with E = L × log₂(R), where L is length and R is the character-pool size.

03

Pattern awareness

Common words, keyboard sequences and repeated characters reduce the practical strength of a password.
